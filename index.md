I am a Ph.D. student in the [School of Computing Science](https://www.sfu.ca/computing.html) at Simon Fraser University, advised by [Prof. Jiangchuan Liu](https://www.cs.sfu.ca/~jcliu/). I received my bachelor's degree in Computing Science from Simon Fraser University.

My research focuses on LLM systems, retrieval-augmented generation, live video analytics, and edge–cloud computing. Recently, I have been working on democratizing LLM services through decentralized edge collaboration, and on knowledge-adaptive AI for ecological monitoring in off-grid environments.
